# 1.3.0 材质来源

`birch-knots.png` 为本次通过内置 image_gen 工具生成的木材基础色贴图，已存入本工程并用于木窝、滚轮和后壁。没有把设计稿或生成的场景图充当实际运行截图。

实际生成提示词：

> Generate one production-ready PBR BASE COLOR TEXTURE for a 3D virtual hamster enclosure game. Square 2048x2048 flat orthographic straight-on closeup of a single broad sheet of honey-colored natural birch plywood. Large elegant flowing undulating wood growth rings and 3-4 subtle authentic oval knots, rich fine fibers and subtle pores, natural pale warm beige and light amber colors, matte smooth sanded wood. This represents a 1.5 meter square area of real plywood; grain runs horizontally. Entire image edge to edge is wood, no board seams, no objects, no perspective, no lettering, no vignette, no gradients from lighting, no highlights, no cast shadows, no frame. Photographic detailed material texture with moderate grain contrast, NOT illustration, NOT orange, NOT dark brown. Keep warm beige relatively desaturated for in-game lighting.

提示词中的分辨率是生成请求；游戏使用工具返回的原始 PNG，运行时由 Godot 导入并生成 mipmap。木材光照、木窝和仓鼠模型、动画、垫料堆积、陶器细点与谷物纹路均由工程代码处理。

## 互动配音

`voices/` 的普通话短句由本机 [VoxCPM2](https://github.com/OpenBMB/VoxCPM) 8 位 MLX 模型生成。先根据男童和女童的音色描述合成各自的样本，再用对应的合成样本统一同一角色的台词音色。游戏使用 24 kHz 单声道 WAV，台词和资源的对应关系见 `pet_voice.gd`。
